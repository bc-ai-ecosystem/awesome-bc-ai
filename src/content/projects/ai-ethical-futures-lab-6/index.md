Meeting artifacts from AI+BC's AI Ethical Futures Lab #6, where ~20 members worked through the Government of Canada's call for information on advancing AI transparency.

Includes the full transcript, facilitator notes, a recording, an AI-generated meeting summary, draft survey responses, and short briefs on agent responsibility, incident response, and AI systems disclosure. The lab's consensus: focus less on regulating providers, more on empowering end-users.
