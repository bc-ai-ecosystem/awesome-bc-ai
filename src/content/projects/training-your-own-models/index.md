The complete 40-chapter manuscript with a 243-term glossary, three browser-only learning tools, full-text search, and a 247-page PDF. Honest about its evidence boundary: documented capabilities, worked calculations, and proposed starting configs — an educational resource, not a benchmark report. Companion book to BC + AI's Local AI Fundamentals course.
