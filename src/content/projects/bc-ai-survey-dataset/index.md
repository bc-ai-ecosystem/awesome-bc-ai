Survey data from 1,001 British Columbians on artificial intelligence: 17 core questions covering AI experience, job impact, creative impact, sector applications, governance, and future vision, with rich demographics and 5,000+ text responses with sentiment analysis.

Ships with a data dictionary, an explore-data starter script, and the winning hackathon prototypes built on top of it.
