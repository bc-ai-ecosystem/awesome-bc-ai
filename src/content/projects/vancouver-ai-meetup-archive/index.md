The public archive of the Vancouver AI Community's monthly meetups: verbatim transcripts, analytical recaps with key themes and quotes, and structured JSON indexes of events, speakers, and documents.

Built for community hacking — semantic search over meetup history, network visualization of the speaker graph, topic modeling across two years of AI discourse, and quote mining for newsletters and socials.
